Our Mission

The Florida Cave Survey is dedicated to the systematic documentation, scientific study, and preservation of Florida's unique cave systems and karst landscapes. We are committed to promoting responsible cave conservation through comprehensive mapping, research, and education while fostering positive relationships with landowners, government agencies, and the broader community.

Our mission encompasses protecting Florida's diverse cave-dwelling wildlife, including critically important bat populations and other specialized cave fauna that depend on these fragile underground ecosystems. We practice and advocate for exemplary stewardship that safeguards sensitive species and their habitats while advancing scientific understanding of Florida's subterranean environments.

Through respectful collaboration with private landowners and public land managers, we work to balance exploration, research, and preservation. We believe that by building trust, conducting careful documentation, and promoting responsible cave practices, we can ensure these irreplaceable natural treasures and their wildlife inhabitants are protected for future generations while maintaining access for legitimate scientific and educational purposes.

The Florida Cave Survey serves as a bridge between the scientific community, conservation organizations, and property owners, working together to protect Florida's remarkable underground frontier and the unique life it sustains.
